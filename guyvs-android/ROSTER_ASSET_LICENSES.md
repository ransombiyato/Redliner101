# Built-in Roster Asset Policy

The built-in Guy roster may use only non-identifiable stock portraits or other assets whose reuse terms have been checked and recorded. To avoid portraying identifiable people in a fighting context, this roster will prefer mannequins, objects, abstract figures, and original geometric overlays.

| Source | Reuse basis | Project rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) | Pexels states that photos can be used free of charge, modified, and used in apps; it forbids using identifiable people in an offensive context, implying endorsement, or reselling unaltered copies. | Use only non-identifiable subjects; preserve the individual source page in this record. |
| [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) | Pixabay permits free use and adaptation subject to prohibited uses and warns that other rights may apply. | Use only non-identifiable subjects without brands, logos, or third-party protected material. |
| [Wikimedia Commons Licensing](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Licensing) | Commons requires each media description page to declare a free license or public-domain status. | Use only files with a directly documented public-domain, CC0, CC BY, or compatible license, and include any required attribution. |

The roster does not include SpennTF2 source images, character art, channel branding, or exact character roster.
